Things I've made that stuck

  • Co-authored YOLOv8 and the ultralytics package, the most popular open-source computer-vision framework, running everywhere from research labs to production edge devices.
  • Shipped YOLOv5-seg, the world's first instance-segmentation model to run in real time on CPU.
  • Co-authored stable-worldmodel, a platform for reproducible world-model research, with Yann LeCun, Randall Balestriero, and others.
  • Built search and retrieval infrastructure used across the RAG ecosystem: rerankers, hybrid search, and multimodal retrieval in LanceDB.
